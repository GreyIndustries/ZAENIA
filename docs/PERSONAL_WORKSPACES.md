## Workbench

Create a project, select its subject, color, card size and stage, and save its notes. Subjects: Science, Biology, Tech, Bio tech, Bio chem, Nuclear physics, Nano technology and AI. Project cards form a forum-style board. Subject and title filters apply to the current page; use More/Previous for additional pages.

Draw with a mouse, finger or stylus. Choose freehand or straight lines, undo strokes, set board width/units and export SVG. The board is 1,000 × 600 logical units; physical height is 0.6 × width, and last-stroke length follows that scale. Export dimensions are converted to millimeters. These are concept measurements, not CAD tolerances or a solved mechanical model.

Keep CAD file/link references and import PNG/JPEG sketch exports (under 600 KB, at most 16 megapixels). The reference is stored locally and included in an encrypted profile export. Keep original CAD/artwork files separately. Record hypotheses, conditions and actual simulation results in the scenario field. ZAE does not claim a simulation has run merely because a result was typed here.

## Music

Track GarageBand project references, BPM, key, session notes and stages from Idea to Released. Add HTTPS release links for YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify and Apple Music. GarageBand references do not launch or edit the Apple application on Windows.

Record dated manual statistics with the platform, metric, value, source and reporting period. Views, plays, listeners, likes and saves are distinct; do not combine them into a fabricated total. Automatic authenticated artist dashboards are not connected by this change.

## Fitness & Health

Log each consumed serving's calories, protein and fiber with its nutrition source. Log exercises with duration, active calories and an estimate source. Edit a saved entry, or archive it to remove it from totals without losing the record. Daily totals include every matching active log, including pages beyond the first 100.

- Intake minus exercise = logged food calories − logged active exercise calories.
- Estimated energy balance = logged food calories − logged active exercise calories − the optional non-exercise daily expenditure estimate.

The first number is not a full calorie deficit. Do not enter a device's total daily expenditure as a non-exercise baseline and then count its exercise again. Targets are owner supplied; no calorie requirement, protein/fiber target, diagnosis or weight-loss prescription is inferred. Logs reflect entered data, not unrecorded meals or activity. See the [NIH/NIDDK energy-planning information](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/body-weight-planner) for the distinction between intake and activity-based expenditure.

Create a training draft from pasted printed text, a video transcript, a public text article or a TXT/Markdown/PDF/DOCX/SRT/VTT file under 1 MB. PDF/Word extraction runs locally; a scanned page without readable text needs OCR or manual transcription. Video URLs are kept as references, with an explicit message that they have not been watched. Paste captions to extract their prescriptions. Review/edit movements, sets/reps/durations and schedule before saving. Mark steps done, then Record session / reset checklist to retain a dated snapshot. Save the plan to persist that history. Log exercise separately for duration/calorie accounting.

## Calendar completion

The requested browser and CarPlay briefs can ask about one recently ended timed event. They read today/yesterday and consider an end within the preceding 24 hours; a missing end uses the scheduled start. Overnight end times are handled. All-day and unknown-time events are excluded. "Completed", "done", "finished" or an affirmative answer mark that occurrence complete; "not yet" leaves it unfinished. Questions do not repeat within 12 hours after delivery/answer. The question is specific to the event, without a generic follow-up invitation.

Completion survives restart and encrypted profile restore. Later recurring occurrences have different identities. Playing audio never marks an event complete. Calendar → Briefing completion checks shows the selected day's records; Reopen corrects a mistaken completion using the record revision. This does not delete the source event or alter Google Calendar.
