# Research and News

Three Research tabs in the left sidebar, plus the News tab. Everything is analysed by ZAE's own brain on your PC; only search words go online.

## Library
Search **Project Gutenberg**, **Open Library** and the **Internet Archive** at once. Free-to-read books are listed first and highlighted.
- **Read & scan here** downloads a free book's text (Gutenberg or Internet Archive only) and has ZAE say what it's about, its main ideas, people, places and dates, and whether it's worth reading in full.
- **Open in browser** and **Add to notebook** work on any result.
- Books that aren't free show how to borrow them, or find them in a library.
- The side pane shows a Wikipedia overview, plus links to HathiTrust, the Library of Congress, WorldCat, Google Books and Wikisource.

## Historical connections
Type up to three words, separated by commas, then pick a source:
- **US newspapers, 1770s–1960s** (Library of Congress, Chronicling America);
- **Books and documents, 1700s–2020s** (Internet Archive);
- **World online news since 2017** (GDELT).

The bars show how often each word was written about, per decade (or per year for news), relative to how much was printed. Click a bar to see what was written then (When / What / Where). **What happened then?** has ZAE explain the context, saying which parts come from the samples.

## Notebook
Make a topic, then add sources with **Add to notebook** (from Library, News or Historical connections) or by pasting. Notes save as you type. Her analysis buttons:
- Summary
- Compare sources
- Claims vs evidence
- Timeline
- Patterns & causes
- What to research next

**Save to Notes** keeps the notes, her analysis and the source list as a ZAE note.

## News
Tech and world headlines from 16 newsrooms, with topic pills: World, US, Science, AI, Hardware, Security, Gaming.
- **Search world news** finds articles from news sites everywhere (GDELT, English, the last 1–30 days).
- Click any story to open the reader: **Read article** (the text without ads), **Summarise**, **Facts vs opinion**, **Ask ZAE**, **Open in browser**, **Add to notebook**.

## Safety
- Free, legal, public sources only.
- Book, page and news text is untrusted data: ZAE reads it and never follows instructions in it.
- Pages are fetched only from public internet addresses, never this PC, your home network or Tailscale.
- Book text is only downloaded from Gutenberg and the Internet Archive.
- The notebook is in `data/research/notebook.json` on this PC.
